# Code of conduct

dockudos is built by people who want documentation to be easy to write and pleasant to read. We want
the project's spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any chat we run) to be
welcoming to everyone who takes part in good faith.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be patient with questions. Everyone was new once, and docs tools are how many people first meet MDX.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable, and take feedback without defensiveness.
- Respect people's time: search existing issues first, and keep reports focused.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory jokes or language, whether in public or in private.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or attention that was not invited.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately misleading others.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public. It can also
apply to behavior outside those spaces if it harms a member of the community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people
from the project's spaces when behavior breaks these rules. We aim to warn first when the problem is
minor, and to act at once when it is not.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately. Use the repository's private vulnerability and
conduct reporting option on GitHub if it is enabled, or the contact details on the maintainers'
profiles. Reports are kept confidential, and maintainers who are part of a report do not take part in
deciding what to do about it.
