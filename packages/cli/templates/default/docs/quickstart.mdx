---
title: Quickstart
description: Add a page and put it in the sidebar.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a page">
    Add `docs/my-page.mdx` with a `title` in its frontmatter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it to the sidebar">
    List `my-page` under a group in `docs.json`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    ```package-install
    npm run build
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pages that are not listed in `docs.json` still work; they just are not in the sidebar.
