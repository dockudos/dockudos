---
title: Billing
description: Plans, invoices, and how to change your billing details.
---

Billing is managed per organization in the dashboard under **Settings, then Billing**. Only owners and billing admins can see it.

## Plans

The Free plan includes 1,000 requests a day. The Team plan includes 100,000 requests a day and priority support. The Enterprise plan is custom.

You can change plans at any time. An upgrade takes effect immediately and is prorated. A downgrade takes effect at the end of the billing period.

## Change the billing email

Open Settings, then Billing, and edit the email address that receives invoices. Invoices and payment failure notices are sent to this address, not to the owner's login email.

## Invoices

Invoices are generated on the first day of each month and emailed as PDFs. Past invoices can be downloaded from the Billing page.

## Cancel your subscription

Choose Cancel subscription on the Billing page. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, then the organization moves to the Free plan. Your data is kept for thirty days after a cancellation.
