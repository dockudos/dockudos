title: Section

---

`Section` builds [landing pages](../guides/layouts.mdx#landing-and-marketing-pages): stack bands on a page with `layout: landing`. Each band
holds a title, a lead sentence and anything else.

```mdx
---
title: Acme
layout: landing
---

<Section
  tone="primary"
  align="center"
  title="Docs that answer back"
  lead="Search, ask and read in one place."
>
  <LinkButton href="/getting-started">Start</LinkButton>
</Section>

<Section title="Why Acme" id="why">
  <Columns cols={3}>
    <Tile title="Fast">Builds in seconds.</Tile>
    <Tile title="Open">Plain files you own.</Tile>
    <Tile title="Smart">Ask the docs.</Tile>
  </Columns>
</Section>

<Section tone="muted" title="Numbers">
  <Stats items={[{ label: 'Pages', value: '1,200' }]} />
</Section>
```

On a page with another layout it is the same band, inside the content width. In the Markdown twin it is a `##` heading, the lead sentence and the content.
