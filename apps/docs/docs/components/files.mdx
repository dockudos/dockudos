<Files>
  <Folder name="docs" defaultOpen>
    <File name="index.mdx" />
    <Folder name="guides">
      <File name="writing.mdx" />
    </Folder>
  </Folder>
  <File name="docs.json" />
  <File name="next.config.mjs" />
</Files>

```mdx
<Files>
  <Folder name="docs" defaultOpen>
    <File name="index.mdx" />
    <Folder name="guides">
      <File name="writing.mdx" />
    </Folder>
  </Folder>
  <File name="docs.json" />
</Files>
```

Folders open and close with Enter or Space. `defaultOpen` starts one open.

## Custom icons

`File` and `Folder` take an `icon`: any element, such as an SVG, an `<img>` or an icon component.
`Folder` also takes `openIcon`, shown while it is open; without it `icon` is used for both states.

<Files>
  <Folder
    name="src"
    defaultOpen
    icon={<span>📁</span>}
    openIcon={<span>📂</span>}
  >
    <File name="app.ts" icon={<span>🟦</span>} />
    <File name="notes.md" />
  </Folder>
</Files>

```mdx
<Files>
  <Folder
    name="src"
    defaultOpen
    icon={<MyFolder />}
    openIcon={<MyFolderOpen />}
  >
    <File name="app.ts" icon={<img src="/ts.svg" alt="" />} />
  </Folder>
</Files>
```

Icons are decorative: they are hidden from screen readers, and sized to 1rem.
