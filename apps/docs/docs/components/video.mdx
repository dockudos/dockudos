Give one of `src` or `embed`. An embed must be `https://`, is loaded lazily, and runs in a sandbox
that does not allow it to navigate your page. Anything else fails the build.
Captions are your responsibility: add a `<track>` through your own component if the video has speech.

## Behavior

- **A file** is played by the browser's own player: controls, fullscreen, keyboard and captions it supports. It loads only
  its first frame and length until played (`preload="metadata"`). Browsers that cannot play it show a link to download it.
- **An embed** is an `iframe` for sites such as YouTube or Vimeo: give their _embed_ address (`…/embed/ID`), not the page
  address. It loads when scrolled near, is sandboxed so it cannot navigate your page, and allows fullscreen.
- **Printing**: neither player can print, so paper shows "Video: _title_ (_address_)" instead.
- **Not both**: with `embed` and `src` together, `embed` wins.

<Callout type="warning" title="Content security policy">
  If your site sends a `Content-Security-Policy` header, allow the embed host in
  `frame-src`, or the player stays blank.
</Callout>

Make `title` say what the video shows ("Product tour, 2 minutes") and not "video". It is the only name a screen reader has for
the player. See [Frame](./frame.mdx) to give a video a border and a caption.
