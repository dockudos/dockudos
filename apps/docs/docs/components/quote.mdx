<Quote
  author="Ada Lovelace"
  source="Notes on the Analytical Engine"
  href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ada_Lovelace"
>
  The Engine might compose elaborate and scientific pieces of music of any
  degree of complexity.
</Quote>

```mdx
<Quote
  author="Ada Lovelace"
  source="Notes on the Analytical Engine"
  href="https://example.com/notes"
>
  The Engine might compose elaborate and scientific pieces of music.
</Quote>
```

It is a real `figure` with a `blockquote` and a `figcaption`, and `href` sets the quote's `cite`. With an `href` the credit
is a link. Use it for testimonials, pull quotes from an article and sayings from a person; use a plain Markdown `>` quote for
anything that is only text.
